About Me

Crafting Apps,

I'm Subin Tamang, a Flutter & Firebase developer currently pursuing a Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) at Texas College, Nepal. I specialize in building cross-platform mobile applications that are fast, scalable, and intuitive.

My core stack revolves around Flutter, Dart, and Firebase — from authentication and Firestore to real-time databases. I follow Clean Architecture principles and leverage state management patterns like Provider and Bloc to keep codebases maintainable.

Beyond mobile, I've explored Computer Vision with Microsoft Azure and Python-based solutions. I thrive in challenge-driven environments — I even shipped a full app in a single day during a competitive code challenge.
